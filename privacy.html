Privacy Policy for ioPUZZLE

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

At ioPUZZLE (accessible from https://www.iopuzzle.com), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our students, educators, and visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that are collected and recorded by ioPUZZLE and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

Our interactive STEM simulators run directly in your browser. If you contact us directly via support@iopuzzle.com or register for workshops, we may receive your name, email address, grade level, and message contents strictly for educational delivery.

2. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to www.iopuzzle.com and other sites on the internet. However, visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL – https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

3. Children's Information & Student Safety

Protecting children while learning online is core to our mission. ioPUZZLE provides interactive Science, Mathematics, and Coding simulations designed for CBSE learners. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and guide their children's STEM learning activities.

4. Contact Information

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please contact our founder and team at: support@iopuzzle.com
